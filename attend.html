📖 Attendance Guide

What are the buttons for?

🚗
Depart
🏠
Return
Depart / Return
Records travel distance for vehicle fuel support calculation. Distance is measured via Google Maps driving route, not straight-line distance.
☀️
Clock In
🌙
Clock Out
Clock In / Clock Out
Records the start and end time of field work to calculate total working hours.
📍 Distance is calculated based on Google Maps driving directions, reflecting actual road distance — not a straight line between two points.

How does it work?

🚗
① Depart
Press when leaving. Your departure location is recorded.
☀️
② Clock In
Press upon arrival. Records work start time.
📏 Travel distance from origin → destination is calculated
🌙
③ Clock Out
Press when work is finished. Working hours are calculated.
⏱️ Working hours = Clock Out − Clock In
🏠
④ Return
Press after arriving back. Return travel distance is recorded.
📏 Travel distance from destination → home is calculated
